OpenTelemetry Collector & Language SDKs

Point your existing otel-collector at ingest.pulseops-otel.io:4317.

Collector YAML

exporters:
  otlp/pulseops:
    endpoint: ingest.pulseops-otel.io:4317
    headers:
      x-pulse-key: ${PULSE_API_KEY}

Rust tracing

opentelemetry_otlp::new_exporter()
  .tonic()
  .with_endpoint("https://ingest.pulseops-otel.io:4317");

Docker Compose

image: pulseops/agent:v2.8.1
command: ['--ebpf-profile', '--interval=15s']
